How to Read & Use the Periodic Table

Anatomy of an Element Cell

  • Atomic Number: Protons in nucleus. Defines element identity.
  • Chemical Symbol: Standard 1-2 letter international notation.
  • Element Name: Official IUPAC name.
  • Atomic Mass: Weighted average of protons + neutrons in amu.

Periods vs. Groups & Valency

Periods (Rows 1–7)

Share the same number of electron energy shells. Moving left to right adds 1 proton & 1 electron per element.

Groups (Columns 1–18)

Possess identical valence electron counts, producing similar chemical reactivity!

Valency Guidelines

  • Group 1: Valency of 1+ ($Na^+$)
  • Group 2: Valency of 2+ ($Ca^{2+}$)
  • Group 17: Valency of 1- ($Cl^-$)
Mastery Guide — Module 2

Periodic Trends & Core Rules

Atomic Radius

Increases DOWN a group (adding shell layers); Decreases LEFT to RIGHT across a period (stronger nuclear pull).

Electronegativity

Increases UP and RIGHT. Fluorine (F) is the most electronegative element.

Ionization Energy

Energy required to strip an electron. Increases UP and RIGHT. Helium (He) holds the highest value.

The Metalloid Staircase Line

The diagonal line (B, Si, Ge, As, Sb, Te, At) separates metals on the left from nonmetals on the right. Metalloids display semiconductor properties vital for modern microchips.
